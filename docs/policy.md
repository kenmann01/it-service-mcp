# Equipment request policy

This policy says which equipment requests are approved, which are denied, and which are sent to IT for a decision. It covers headphones, phones, and laptops.

To submit a request, give your name, your role, the item, and the reason. Roles are employee, manager, and director. Director means anyone above manager.

An approved request is issued under the rule below. A denied request is refused by this policy; the rule below says why. An escalated request is not approved and not refused by this policy. IT reviews it.

## Headphones

Every employee, manager, and director may request headphones.

- A replacement for the pair you have is approved.
- A first pair, when you do not have headphones yet, is approved.
- A second or spare pair, while you keep the pair you already have, is denied.

## Phones

Employees may request a replacement for a company phone they already have. That replacement is approved. Any other phone request from an employee is not approved: a first company phone is escalated, and an extra phone is denied.

Managers and directors may request one company phone.

- A replacement for the phone you have is approved.
- A first company phone, when you do not have one yet, is approved.
- A second phone, when you already have one, is denied.

## Laptops

A laptop request is denied. This policy does not approve laptops.

- A manager or director who asks to keep the new laptop and the old one for a week, and then return the old one to IT, is escalated. Keeping both laptops is a human decision.
- An employee who asks for a laptop is denied. This policy does not say what an employee may have.
- Any other laptop request from a manager or director is denied.

## Requests this policy does not cover

IT escalates a request when any of the following is true.

- Your name, role, item, or reason is missing.
- Your role is not employee, manager, or director.
- The item is not headphones, a phone, or a laptop.
- A headphones or phone request does not say whether it is a replacement, a first one, or a second one.
